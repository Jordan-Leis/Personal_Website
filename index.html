about.md

                

                    

                    

                    

                

            

            

                

                    

                        
I like to build.

                        
I'm a 3B Electrical Engineering student at the University of Waterloo. I've built at every layer I've needed to: an RL controller and the paper behind it, RAG pipelines over 300-page regulatory documents, a Qt/C++ motion-control app, PCBs through EMI/EMC compliance, and now pipelined fixed-point accelerators in SystemVerilog. Not to collect skills. The problem kept moving.


                        
I got here from the other direction. I spent two years doing AI research at Wat.AI, leading an 11-person team building a reinforcement learning controller for off-grid microgrids in rural sub-Saharan Africa. It worked: 23% less diesel per year at equal reliability, and a first-authored paper accepted at CUCAI 2026.


                        
Then I looked at deploying it. The low-cost board we needed to run the controller had roughly doubled in price and was unavailable, squeezed by the AI compute shortage. The inference compute cost more than parts of the microgrid it was supposed to control. The algorithm was never the bottleneck. The hardware was. And when compute gets scarce, the people with the least get cut off first.


                        
I called it a pivot. It isn't. I started at the application, hit a wall made of hardware, and went one layer down to work on the wall.


                        
Right now the bottleneck is hardware, so that's where I am: working through the ECE 124 → ECE 222 → ECE 327 Digital Hardware Systems sequence, closing timing through architecture instead of tool flags. If the bottleneck moves again, I'll move with it. Before this: ML Engineer (Research) at Health Canada's PMRA; an experiment I designed at the National Research Council with roughly $100,000 of lab equipment specced for it; TAing ECE 150 for 400+ first-years.


                        
Cansbridge Fellow, 1 of 15 selected nationally — Asia internship Winter 2027. VP Services, Waterloo Engineering Society.

🔲

                            
MVM Accelerator

                            
SystemVerilog • Zynq UltraScale+ MPSoC

                            
128-lane fixed-point matrix-vector engine running 1024 MACs per cycle at 450 MHz, sustaining ~850 GOPS. Same architecture family Microsoft built BrainWave on. Timing closed entirely through RTL structure — register fanout trees, an address tree with fanout 1 — with no synthesis flags, XDC hacks, or phys_opt_design.

                            

                                SystemVerilog
                                Vivado
                                DSP48E2
                                Timing Closure
                            

                        


                        

                            
📈

                            
tanh Accelerator

                            
SystemVerilog • Fixed-Point Pipeline

                            
Pipelined Q2.12 tanh core with a ready/valid streaming interface, driven from 280 MHz to 840 MHz across four rewrites. The last gain came from registering every inter-DSP link, moving the design from routing-bound to logic-bound. Verified against a golden model with randomized stimulus and mid-stream backpressure testing.

                            

                                SystemVerilog
                                DSP48E2
                                Fixed-Point
                                Verification
                            

                        


                        

                            
🟩

                            
Wordle on Kria

                            
SystemVerilog • Hardware/Software Co-Design

                            
Wordle running in FPGA fabric on a Xilinx Kria board. Game logic is a 5-state FSM in programmable logic; a baremetal C application on the ARM processing system drives it over seven AXI-GPIO blocks and UART. Vivado block design generated from Tcl, exported to Vitis via XSA handoff.

                            

                                SystemVerilog
                                AXI-GPIO
                                Vitis
                                Baremetal C
                                FSM

Microgrid RL Controller

                            
first author • CUCAI 2026

                            
Reinforcement learning controller for off-grid hybrid microgrids in rural sub-Saharan Africa. Most work in the field treats electricity as a commodity to sell back to a grid; this treated it as a scarce resource, optimizing how many people one generator can reach. A 150-run benchmark on five years of NASA POWER climate data found DDPG optimal: 23% less diesel per year at equal reliability. Led an 11-person team. First-authored, accepted at CUCAI 2026.

                            

                                Reinforcement Learning
                                TensorFlow
                                Gymnasium
                                NASA POWER
